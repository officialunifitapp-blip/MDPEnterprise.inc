## 1. Cold Call

**Opener (survives "not interested"):**
> "Hey [FIRST NAME] — this isn't a pitch and I'll be quick. I know a water job doesn't wait till morning. When a call comes in at 2am and goes to voicemail, that job's gone to whoever picked up. Real reason I called: I built a phone line that answers those after-hours emergency calls for you and books them. You can call it yourself and hear it — takes 90 seconds. Want the number?"

If "not interested":
> "Fair — most guys say that till they hear it handle a real 2am water call. Two minutes, call the number, decide for yourself. Worst case you hang up. Cool?"

**Voicemail (one line):**
> "[FIRST NAME], it's [YOUR NAME]. Built a line that answers your after-hours emergency calls so you stop losing them to voicemail — call [DEMO #] and hear it handle one. That's it."
