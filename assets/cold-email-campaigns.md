# Cold Email Playbook

Two offers, one system. Direct, blue-collar-friendly, zero corporate fluff.
No fabricated results — we have no proof yet. Lean on: the LIVE demo line
`(314) 887-9824`, a free redesign mockup, and risk-free framing.

Rule of thumb: they buy answered calls, booked jobs, and a site that works on
a phone. Not "AI" or "automation."

---

## A) DELIVERABILITY SETUP — do this BEFORE sending one email

If email lands in spam, nothing else in this doc matters. Set this up first.

**1. Never send from your main domain.**
One spam complaint or a bad list can burn your primary domain and kill your
real business email forever. It is not recoverable. Isolate all cold sending.

**2. Buy 1–2 secondary sending domains.**
- Keep them close to your brand: `getYOURAGENCY.com`, `tryYOURAGENCY.com`,
  `YOURAGENCYhq.com`. Cheap (~$10–12/yr each).
- Point them at your main site (301 redirect) so they look legit.

**3. Create 2–3 inboxes per domain.**
- Use Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 (~$6/inbox/mo). Real inboxes, not
  free aliases.
- Example: `mario@getYOURAGENCY.com`, `m.delpietro@getYOURAGENCY.com`,
  `hello@getYOURAGENCY.com`.
- 2 domains x 3 inboxes = 6 inboxes.

**4. Set SPF, DKIM, and DMARC on every domain.**
- SPF: authorizes who can send for the domain.
- DKIM: signs your mail so it can't be forged.
- DMARC: start at `p=none` to monitor, tighten later.
- Your sending tool + Google/MS have click-through guides. Verify all three
  are green before warmup. No exceptions.

**5. Warm up every inbox for 2–3 weeks BEFORE real sends.**
- Turn on automated warmup in your sending tool (built into Instantly and
  Smartlead).
- It sends/receives small volumes between real inboxes, opens, and marks as
  important — builds sender reputation.
- Do not skip this. Cold-blasting a fresh domain = instant spam folder.

**6. Pick a sending tool: Instantly or Smartlead.**
- Both do warmup, inbox rotation, and spintax. Either is fine. Pick one.
- Connect all 6 inboxes. Enable inbox rotation so volume spreads across them.

**7. Keep volume realistic: 20–30 emails per inbox per day.**
- 6 inboxes x 25 = ~150 new prospects/day, ~750/week. Plenty.
- Ramp up over the first 1–2 weeks; don't hit max on day one.

**8. First email: plain text only.**
- No images. No links. No attachments. No logos or signatures with tracking.
- Links and images in a first cold email tank deliverability and scream "blast."
- Turn OFF open-tracking (it injects a pixel that hurts inboxing).
- Save the link/demo number for the ask itself, in plain text.

**9. One clear CTA per email. Always.**
- Two asks = zero replies. Pick one: a yes/no reply, or "call the demo line."

**10. Hygiene.**
- Verify every list (ZeroBounce / MillionVerifier) before loading. Bounces
  above ~3% get you flagged.
- Keep bounce and spam-complaint rates low. Pause and diagnose if replies drop.

## B) TARGETING / ICP

### Offer 1 — Website Creation
- **Who:** local/small service businesses — restoration, roofing, HVAC,
  plumbing, landscaping, auto shops, med spas, dentists, contractors, gyms.
- **Buying trigger (observable):** site is not mobile-friendly, loads slow,
  looks 2012, has no site (Facebook page only), broken links, or a "under
  construction" page.
- **How to find them:**
  - Google Maps scraping (e.g. a Maps scraper by city + trade). Pull name,
    website, phone.
  - Open each site on your phone. If it's ugly, slow, or missing — it's a lead.
    That firsthand look becomes your `{{observed_problem}}`.
  - Filter for businesses with reviews (they get traffic) but a weak/no site.

### Offer 2 — AI Automation (receptionist / missed-call text-back / booking)
- **Who:** appointment- and emergency-driven local businesses — restoration,
  HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electricians, garage doors, towing, med spas,
  dental, salons. Anyone who loses money when the phone rings unanswered.
- **Buying trigger (observable):**
  - Google reviews saying "never called back," "no answer," "left voicemail,
    heard nothing."
  - Solo operators / small crews who are on a job (can't answer) during the day.
  - After-hours emergencies going to voicemail.
- **How to find them:**
  - Apollo for owner name + email by industry and headcount (aim ~2–20 staff).
  - Google Maps + read the 1–3 star reviews for the "nobody answered" quote —
    that verbatim line is your best opener.

## E) RULES + CADENCE

**Follow-up cadence (per prospect):**
- Email 1: Day 0
- Email 2: Day 3
- Email 3: Day 7
- Email 4 (breakup): Day 12
- Then stop. Move on. Re-add to a new angle in 60–90 days if you want.

Send Tue–Thu, mornings local time, best. Reply in the same thread for
follow-ups (keeps context, lifts reply rate).

**Rules:**
- One CTA per email. One.
- First email: plain text, no links/images. (Offer 2's demo number is plain
  text, which is fine — it's not a clickable link.)
- Under 110 words. Skimmable on a phone.
- Personalize the first line with a real, observed detail. Generic = deleted.
- Reply within the hour when someone answers. Speed closes.
- Log every objection to `objections.md` the same day.

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't send from your main domain.
- Don't skip warmup.
- Don't fabricate case studies, client names, or results — we don't have them
  yet. Use the demo, the free mockup, and month-to-month risk reversal instead.
- Don't say "AI," "automation," "synergy," "solution," or "circle back."
- Don't ask two things at once.
- Don't attach anything or paste a logo in email 1.
- Don't buy a scraped list and blast it unverified.
- Don't chase past the breakup email.
