## What this file says after 15 conversations

Two objections carry almost all the weight:

1. **"We answer our own phones"** — ~90% of refusals. The offer already has the
   right answer built into it (Missed Call Recovery agrees with him). It is not
   being used, because none of these calls recorded a response at all.
2. **"We're already talking to someone else"** — CLEAN Restoration, Heartland,
   Innovative. Three of the furthest-progressed leads in the pipeline. Every one
   of them was already shopping. That is not a coincidence: the leads who engage
   are the ones already aware they have this problem.

The pattern under both: 15 conversations produced 0 appointments, and not one
of them has a recorded response. Fill the middle column on the next call — the
right-hand column cannot be written from an objection alone.
