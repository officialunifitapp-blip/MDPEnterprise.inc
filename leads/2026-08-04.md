## Part 3 — Objection Tree (shared, use on every call — unchanged, verified against business-context.md; no new real objections logged yet in objections.md)

**Branch: "Not interested"**
- Objection: "Not interested." / "We're good."
- Response: "Fair enough — most guys say that before they've heard it cost them a job. Can I ask, when's the last time a call went to voicemail after hours? That's the only thing I'm fixing. 30 seconds, then I'll leave you alone."
- Leads to: If they engage → back to Demo Offer. If they shut it down again → "No problem, I'll text you the live demo number in case that changes — (314) 887-9824." → End call, log as cold, follow up in 30 days.

**Branch: "Just send me info"**
- Objection: "Just send me some info" / "Email me something."
- Response: "I can, but info doesn't show you anything — hearing it does. It's a live number, (314) 887-9824, call it right now from your cell, takes 90 seconds, you'll hear it answer, text back, and book a job on the spot. Way faster than reading a PDF."
- Leads to: If they call it live → stay on the line, walk to Close ("If that sounded like it'd catch calls you're missing now, first 3 clients get setup waived — want to lock in this week?"). If they still insist on info only → text the demo number + one-line pitch, log as warm follow-up in 3-5 days, do not fabricate a written case study or results in that follow-up text.

**Branch: "We already have someone answering"**
- Objection: "We've got that covered" / "My wife/office manager answers" / "We have an answering service already."
- Response: "Good — then you already know how much that saves you. Quick gut check: does that person catch every call at 2am, or does some go to voicemail when they're off shift or on another line? This isn't replacing them, it's the backup for when they can't pick up."
- Leads to: If they admit gaps → Demo Offer ("Let's put it on the same nights they're off — 30 seconds, (314) 887-9824"). If they insist it's fully covered → "Respect that. If it ever cracks during a storm surge, keep the number — (314) 887-9824." → log as low-priority, revisit next storm season.

**Branch: "How much is it"**
- Objection: "What's this cost?" / "How much is it?"
- Response: "$500 a month, month-to-month, cancel anytime — no contract lock-in. Normally $500 to set up, but I'm waiving that for the next few shops I bring on this week in exchange for being able to use the results. One missed emergency call usually costs more than a month of this."
